## Design Specificity Verdict

The result is structurally competent but visually category-interchangeable. Lavender selection is coherent, yet the cards, rail, controls, charts, and drawers read as a tidy starter admin rather than the supplied Operator’s Field Guide. The strongest missed expression is structural: the reference’s three-card hierarchy, one dominant payment-activity story, and recent-activity follow-through are absent.

The detector found zero issues in `apps/web`. Browser evidence corroborates accessible named chart landmarks, route-backed payment/customer drawers, visible lavender selected rows, and no console warnings. These are implementation strengths, but they do not measure visual parity. Browser overlay injection was unavailable because the available browser surface exposes read-only evaluation only.

## Overall Impression

The dashboard is calm, dependable, and coherent in interaction. It does not yet tell the operator where to look or what to do first. The highest-leverage improvement is to change the overview from two equal trend panels into one operational story with a supporting recent-activity table.

## What's Working

- The desktop rail in `apps/web/src/components/app-shell.tsx` creates stable location and clear selected-navigation contrast.
- Shared DataTable usage in `apps/web/src/pages/purchases-page.tsx` and `apps/web/src/pages/customers-page.tsx` produces compact records with readable status chips and selected-row continuity.
- `apps/web/src/components/payment-details.tsx` and `apps/web/src/components/customer-details.tsx` group record data with accessible labels and tabs; the route-backed drawer preserves list context.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Overview hierarchy does not match the target story

`apps/web/src/pages/dashboard-page.tsx` renders two peer `TrendCard`s after the metrics. The comp calls for one dominant payment-activity panel plus a recent-activity table.

**Why it matters:** no primary operational question is established, and the page repeats aggregate totals rather than moving from trend evidence into action.

**Fix:** compose a full-width primary analytics panel and a shared DataTable-based recent-activity section below it. Preserve the current TanStack chart implementation, its styling, and planned gradient work.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout apps/web`

### [P1] The rail advertises inactive product areas and is too dense

`apps/web/src/components/app-shell.tsx` shows seven disabled analytics/chargeback items beside only three working routes.

**Why it matters:** false affordances and a scrollable inner rail add cognitive load before an operator begins a task.

**Fix:** make the compact rail express the finished, supported work. If breadth must be shown, group unavailable destinations under an explicitly non-interactive label. No new primitive is needed.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill apps/web`

### [P1] Desktop tables hide critical scan context

`apps/web/src/pages/purchases-page.tsx` and `apps/web/src/pages/customers-page.tsx` render nine to ten columns; Protection, 3DS, and later fields are cut off to the right without a strong cue or pinned identity context.

**Why it matters:** an operator cannot compare records confidently without horizontal hunting.

**Fix:** add named shared DataTable responsive behavior in `packages/ui`—for example, pinned identity columns and an explicit overflow affordance—then apply it to both routes.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout apps/web`

### [P2] Metric cards lack the target’s differentiated scan signal

`SummaryCard` in `apps/web/src/pages/dashboard-page.tsx` maps directly to neutral `Card variant="metric"`.

**Why it matters:** payments, customers, and payouts have equal visual weight but insufficient distinctiveness for fast scanning.

**Fix:** add named optional metric icon/trend slots to shared `packages/ui` Card and use restrained lavender emphasis on Overview.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout apps/web`

### [P2] Detail drawers feel sparse and card-fragmented

`apps/web/src/components/payment-details.tsx` and `apps/web/src/components/customer-details.tsx` leave the wide drawer feeling like disconnected blocks; Customer Overview has substantial unused space.

**Why it matters:** the drawer preserves navigation context but does not yet guide an investigation or signal a next step.

**Fix:** consolidate facts into stronger sections, move status/context into a compact header, and use shared Drawer/Tabs. If repeated layout is warranted, promote a named `DetailSection` or `KeyValueList` into `packages/ui`.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout apps/web`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Ops analyst:** no clear exception or priority signal identifies a transaction/customer requiring attention.
- **Finance reviewer:** headline totals arrive without enough reconciliation or period-comparison context.
- **Support investigator:** Customer Overview is thin before Activity or Methods is explored; no next investigative action is apparent.
- **Mobile operator:** the wide data set becomes a horizontal-scroll hunt unless identity/context remains anchored.

## Minor Observations

- Record counts are useful but visually weak as a third subtitle line; make them part of table context.
- Status display copy is lowercase while headers and titles are sentence case.
- Date filters are isolated at upper-right instead of sharing a consistent control group with page context.
- The persistent inner rail scrollbar reads as uncurated navigation volume.

## Questions to Consider

1. Is the primary persona a merchant operator or a cross-merchant Coinflow admin? “Across every merchant” changes what belongs in the first chart and table.
2. What single decision should the first viewport enable: exception triage, payment-growth assessment, or record finding?
3. Are disabled rail destinations necessary to demonstrate platform breadth, or are they masking a smaller finished product?
